My Application

Anyone who plays Diablo II—whether the original version or the “Resurrected” version—is familiar with this process. You plan to create a new character and, along with that, a new build, and you think about which pieces of equipment you need to collect overall and which ones you already own. My web application is designed to provide a little help with this.

Runes

Runes are important in this game for crafting special, particularly powerful equipment. This is done through so-called…

Rune Words

A rune word combines specific runes in a fixed order and places them in a suitable blank item that has the correct number of sockets. This creates powerful…

Items

Sure, rune words are among the strongest items in the game, but there are also unique items and set items on the collection list.

Mercenaries

Every hero has a loyal companion whose equipment should be managed, just as…

Hero

…as that of the hero himself.

My software helps manage runes, rune words, items, mercenaries, and the hero himself as you collect them.

Use of AI

I used AI to create my CSS document and to generate the image content. Since English is not my native language, I also used it occasionally to translate from German to English.
